# Approved Maxbet delivery roadmap

Provided by the owner on 1 October 2026. Continue in this order in the existing monorepo. Detailed requirements are refined within each phase; this sequence does not authorize inventing production schemas ahead of the relevant design work.

1. **Project Foundation** — architecture, repository, tech stack, environments, conventions, reliability foundations.
2. **Brand & Visual System** — logo/brand application, colors, typography, UI style, reusable design system.
3. **Customer-Facing Frontend** — public website, catalogue experience, navigation, responsive customer interface.
4. **Customer Account & Verification** — registration, approval workflow, login, account profile, FEMSOL customer linking.
5. **Admin Frontend** — staff dashboard, admin navigation, operational interfaces and management screens.
6. **Product System** — product catalogue, categories, brands, product information, images and visibility.
7. **Inventory System** — stock availability, reservations, inventory states and synchronization rules.
8. **FEMSOL Integration** — Maxbet Local Bridge, mappings, synchronization, queues, outage handling and reconciliation.
9. **Order System** — carts, checkout, order creation, revisions, fulfillment and FEMSOL invoice linkage.
10. **Order Status System** — order lifecycle, status transitions, customer/staff tracking and history.
11. **Pricing System** — wholesale prices, customer-specific pricing, discounts and pricing controls.
12. **Communication System** — centralized notifications, in-app alerts, email, WhatsApp and SMS.
13. **Payment System** — Pay Now, manual verification, MoMo/payment-provider integrations, POD and credit-payment workflows.
14. **Invoices & Documents** — invoices, receipts, credit notes, downloadable documents and FEMSOL document linkage.
15. **User & Permission System** — POS, Finance/Admin, Management and Super Admin roles with granular permissions.
16. **Database & Backend** — finalized production data architecture, APIs, migrations, validation and backend business logic.
17. **Security** — authentication hardening, trusted devices, re-authentication, RLS, auditing and security controls.
18. **PWA** — installation, service workers, caching, mobile experience and appropriate offline/degraded behavior.
19. **Search & Performance** — catalogue search, filtering, indexing, caching and application optimization.
20. **Business Intelligence, Financial Analytics & AI** — executive dashboards, profitability, inventory/customer/credit analytics, financial analysis, forecasting and the future **Maxbet Business Analyst**.
21. **Settings** — operational configuration, integrations, notification settings, system health and backup/recovery controls.
22. **Testing** — unit/integration/end-to-end testing, financial correctness, FEMSOL/outage scenarios and recovery testing.
23. **Deployment** — Vercel production deployment, Supabase configuration, domain/DNS, environment variables and production infrastructure.
24. **Final Polish** — UX refinement, responsive checks, accessibility, performance, copy, error states and final QA.
25. **Launch** — production readiness, staff/customer onboarding, monitoring, controlled rollout and post-launch checks.
